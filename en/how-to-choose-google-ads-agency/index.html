How to choose a Google Ads agency in Quebec

A guide built on criteria you can verify before you sign — with any agency, including us.

Big agency, freelancer or small agency: the label says little. What matters are facts you can ask for and check. Here are eight criteria, and how Pixby answers each one.

Worth a close look: when fees are a percentage of your ad budget, the agency has a financial interest in that budget going up. That says nothing about the quality of its work, but ask how pay is calculated and whether you understand the incentive.

5 questions to ask before you sign

  1. “Who will actually manage my account?” Ask for a name and a way to reach that person. A vague answer is a signal worth noting.
  2. “Is the Google Ads account in my name if I leave?” Ask what you keep: account, history and data.
  3. “How do you measure my conversions?” Ask whether calls and forms are tracked separately. An agency that measures only one of the two sees only part of your requests.
  4. “What follow-up do I get, and when?” Ask for the frequency, the format, and a sample report.
  5. “Why this commitment length?” Ask for the minimum term, the exit conditions and the reason for that length.

When NOT to hire an agency at all

Be honest with yourself: if you cannot answer the phone during business hours, or if your service has more bad reviews than good ones — fix that first. No campaign repairs a phone that does not get answered. An honest agency will tell you so at the Audit instead of taking your money.
